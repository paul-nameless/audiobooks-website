Convert an EPUB in four steps

1. Add your EPUB

Import the file from the Files app or iCloud, or send it from your browser over Wi-Fi transfer. It shows up ready to convert.

2. Pick a voice

Choose a free on-device voice, or a premium AI voice in one of many languages. Preview any voice on a sample before you commit, so you know how the whole book will sound.

3. Convert the book

On-device voices convert for free, right on your iPhone, with no internet needed. Premium AI narration is a one-time purchase for that book, priced by its length.

4. Listen offline

The finished audiobook joins your library and plays offline, with chapters and your place saved, exactly like an M4B or MP3 book you brought in yourself.

What about privacy?

On-device conversion never leaves your phone. If you choose premium AI narration, the book text is sent to the server, turned into audio, and discarded right after. It is not stored. The full details are in the privacy policy.

Questions

Is converting an EPUB free?

Yes, with on-device voices. That runs on your iPhone at no cost and works offline. Premium AI narration is optional and costs a one-time payment per book.

Does it upload my book?

On-device conversion never leaves your phone. For premium AI narration, the text is sent to the server, turned into audio, and discarded right after. It is not stored.

Can I listen offline after converting?

Yes. Once a book is made, the audio lives on your device and plays offline with chapters, just like any other audiobook in your library.
