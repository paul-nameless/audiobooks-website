Made for M4B audiobooks

M4B is the audiobook cousin of the M4A file. It holds a whole book in one file, with chapter markers and cover art built in. Audiobooks reads all of that, so a book you drop in shows up with its chapters, artwork, and length ready to go.

It plays the file exactly as it is. Nothing gets re-encoded or uploaded, and there is no DRM layer in the way. If it plays on your Mac, it plays here.

Play an M4B file on your iPhone

  • Add the file from Files or AirDrop, or send it from your browser over Wi-Fi transfer.
  • Open Audiobooks. The book lands in your library with its cover and chapter list.
  • Press play. It saves your place, so you can stop and come back right where you left off.

What you can do while you listen

  • Move through chapters, or drop a bookmark anywhere you want to return to.
  • Set a sleep timer, end it at the chapter break, or have it start on its own at your usual bedtime.
  • Change speed from 0.25x up to 4x, with a custom skip of 15 to 60 seconds.
  • Undo an accidental skip, share a clip up to 60 seconds, and check your listening stats.
  • Add a home screen widget for the book you are on.

Questions

Does it support M4B chapters?

Yes. It reads chapters straight from the M4B file or its metadata, so you can jump between chapters and keep your place. Cover art is picked up automatically too.

Can I import M4B files without iTunes?

Yes. Add files from the Files app or AirDrop, or send them from your browser over Wi-Fi. iTunes and iCloud imports work as well.

Is the M4B player free?

Yes. The app is free, with no ads and no subscription. The only optional purchase is premium AI narration when you convert an ebook.
