Audiobooks from your Jellyfin server

The main Jellyfin apps are built around movies and shows. Audiobooks are an afterthought there: no chapter list, no sleep timer, no memory of where you paused. Audiobooks connects to the same server but treats your books like books.

Point it at your server, browse your audiobooks, and stream one over the network. Or download it first so it plays with no connection at all.

Everything a book needs

  • Chapters and bookmarks, with your place saved as you go.
  • A sleep timer that can stop at the chapter break or at bedtime.
  • Speed from 0.25x to 4x, and a skip button you set to 15 to 60 seconds.
  • Your server library sits next to any local M4B or MP3 files, in one place.

Questions

Does it connect to Jellyfin?

Yes. Point the app at your own Jellyfin server and browse your audiobooks. You can stream a book or download it to listen offline.

Why use it instead of the main Jellyfin app?

It is built for audiobooks, not video. You get chapters, bookmarks, a sleep timer, and 0.25x to 4x speed, and your server sits next to any local files in one library.

Is it free?

Yes. The app is free, with no ads and no subscription. The only optional purchase is premium AI narration when you convert an ebook.
