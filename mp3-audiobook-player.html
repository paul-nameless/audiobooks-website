A real player for MP3 books

Most music apps treat an MP3 audiobook like a song: no chapters, no memory of where you stopped, and a mess of tracks in your library. Audiobooks treats it like a book. It reads chapters and cover art from the file, remembers your position, and keeps your books separate from your music.

There is no DRM in the way and nothing is uploaded. The file plays as it is.

Get your MP3s onto your iPhone

  • Import from the Files app, iCloud, or AirDrop.
  • Or open a browser tab and send files over Wi-Fi transfer, no cable needed.
  • Older iTunes file sharing works too.

Made for long listening

  • Bookmarks you can jump back to, and undo for an accidental skip.
  • A sleep timer that can stop at the end of a chapter, or start on its own at bedtime.
  • Speed from 0.25x to 4x, with a skip button you set between 15 and 60 seconds.
  • Edit the title, author, or cover art when a file comes in messy.
  • Sort and filter your library, and see your listening stats over time.

Questions

Does it play MP3 audiobooks with chapters?

Yes. It reads chapters from the file or its metadata, and picks up the cover art on its own. If a file has no chapters, it still plays as one track and keeps your place.

How do I add MP3 files to the app?

Import from the Files app, iCloud, or AirDrop, or send them from your browser over Wi-Fi. Once added, books play offline.

Is it free?

Yes. The app is free, with no ads and no subscription. The only optional purchase is premium AI narration when you convert an ebook.
